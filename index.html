A life built on purpose

I'm Cayden Farris — author, Maxwell certified coach and speaker, educator, and someone who believes deeply that every person carries unrealized potential waiting to be unlocked.

I served a mission in Honduras, where I learned that connection transcends language and that the most powerful thing one person can offer another is genuine belief in who they can become. That conviction has shaped everything I've done since.

Today I teach, write, coach, and speak with one consistent goal: to help people stop waiting for the right moment to become who they're meant to be — and start building that person right now, while they're young enough for it to matter most.
