Last updated: September 2, 2026

What this site collects

caydenfarris.net collects information only when you choose to send it: your name, email address, and message details when you submit the speaking inquiry form, and your email address when you subscribe to the newsletter. Browsing the site requires no account and no personal information.

How it is used

Speaking inquiries are used to reply to you about your event. Newsletter emails are used to send new writing and book updates. Your information is never sold, rented, or shared for marketing by anyone else, and you can unsubscribe from the newsletter at any time.

Services this site relies on

Form submissions are processed and stored by Formspree. The site is served by Cloudflare, which may log standard technical data (such as IP addresses) to deliver and protect the site. This site sets no advertising cookies and runs no third-party ad trackers.

External links

Links to Amazon, Foreman.coach, and social platforms take you to services with their own privacy policies.
